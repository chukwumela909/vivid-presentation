    FAQ

    The usual questions.

    Will callers know it’s AI?
    Most don’t, which is exactly why Vivid discloses it by default on the first turn. You can change the wording; you can’t remove the setting without an explicit policy flag on your account.
    What happens when it doesn’t know?
    It says so, then follows your escalation rule — warm transfer with a spoken summary, a callback booking, or a ticket. It won’t improvise an answer about your refund policy.
    How fast is it, really?
    312 ms median speech-to-speech, 480 ms at p95 on US and EU edges. Tool calls add whatever your API adds — Vivid fills the gap by speaking instead of leaving dead air.
    Can I use my own voice?
    Yes — 90 seconds of clean audio plus recorded consent from the speaker. Cloned voices are locked to your workspace and can’t be exported.
    Where does the audio live?
    In the region you pick — US, EU or AU. PII is redacted before storage, retention is yours to set from 0 to 365 days, and deletion is verifiable through the audit log.
    Does it work with our phone system?
    If it speaks SIP, yes. Native connectors cover Twilio, Vonage and Telnyx; everything else attaches through a trunk with no change to your carrier.
